# Credits

## Art

All sprites are from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Crediting is
optional, but they deserve it.

- Dungeon Pack — ruins, floors, props, the hero and his animations
- Nature Kit — trees, stumps, rocks, grass
- Graveyard Kit — gravestones, iron fences, crypt, lanterns
- Fantasy Town Kit — cart, fountain, broken fence
- Roguelike RPG Pack — the top-down prototype's tiles and houses

The top-down hero is original pixel art drawn in `tools/assets/build_topdown.py`.

`tools/assets/build_assets.py` rescales and colour-grades them; the sky, fog
and the main-menu painting are generated by `tools/assets/build_backdrops.py`.
